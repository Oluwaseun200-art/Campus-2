    ABOUT THIS PROJECT

    Campus navigation,
    one step at a time.

    This web prototype follows the Campus Transportation Routing System proposal: it organizes campus locations, models route connections as a graph and uses Dijkstra’s algorithm to find a minimum-cost path through the available sample network.

    ⓘ

    Prototype data notice
    Map pins and route connections are illustrative and must be verified against current University of Ibadan locations and approved transport routes before real-world use. Live GPS, live arrival times and traffic feeds are not included.
